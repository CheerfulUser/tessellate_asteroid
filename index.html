What you can download

Each object page carries its phase-folded lightcurve with per-bin uncertainties, the full stacked photometry behind it, and a rotatable convex shape model — all downloadable, and the shape model is synchronised to the lightcurve so you can watch which face produces which feature.

Reading a shape model. Most of these asteroids were seen during a single apparition, from one direction. That constrains the shape but not its orientation in space, so the spin axis is assumed rather than measured and every page says so. Convex inversion also cannot represent concavities, so models systematically under-reach the deepest minima. The rotation periods, by contrast, are well determined.
